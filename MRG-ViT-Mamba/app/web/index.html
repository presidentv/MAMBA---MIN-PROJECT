How to read the score

…

The detector ranks clips; it does not deliver verdicts. On the DAiSEE test partition it recovers of disengaged clips at the cost of false alarms, so a flagged clip is a clip worth a human look, not a conclusion about a learner. Always answering “engaged” would score accuracy on that partition while detecting nothing at all, which is why accuracy is the wrong headline here and ROC-AUC is the right one.
