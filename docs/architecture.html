What the ablation found. Removing the Vision Mamba branch entirely raises test macro-F1 from 0.286 to 0.407; removing the geometric branch collapses it to 0.052. The dt ← MRS path flips zero predictions, because post-gating reliability is 0.93 ± 0.049 and a near-constant rescale is absorbed by the learned dt_proj bias. Both results are noise-dominated at 36 training clips.
